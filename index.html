DL / RC PVC Card Print v4 · 6×4 full page

Crop the front and back of a Driving Licence or RC into print-ready CR80 cards on 6×4 in paper. Everything runs in your browser; files are never uploaded.

Edit:

Drag a box to move it and drag a corner to resize it. Each box can use a different page.

In the printer dialog, choose paper size 4×6 in / 10×15 cm, set scale to 100% / Actual size, and turn off "Fit to page". Turn on borderless printing if your printer has it, so nothing is lost at the edges.

Only print documents that belong to you or that you are authorised to print. A printed copy is not a substitute for the original document issued by the RTO. Keep the e-document (DigiLocker / mParivahan) available for verification.
